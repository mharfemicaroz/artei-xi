DAVAO REGION — The Association of Regional Teacher Education Institutions (ARTEI) XI formally welcomed its newly elected officers during an Oath-Taking Ceremony conducted yesterday, October 5, 2026, at the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) XI Regional Office.The ceremony was administered by Dr. Maricar Casquejo, Regional Director of CHED XI, with Dr. Evelyn S. Ecle, ARTEI Adviser, in attendance.
Leading the new team is Dr. Ruby A. Serrano as President, alongside Dr. Armand James Vallejo, VP-Internal; Dr. Donita May Valera, VP-External; Dr. Jeanilyn T. Muico, Secretary; Dr. Charmaine V. Bañes, Assistant Secretary; Dr. Ida Tudy, Treasurer; Dr. Mharfe M. Micaroz, Assistant Treasurer; Dr. Melanie Jeane C. Galvez, Auditor; Dr. Albert G. Musico, Business Manager; and Dr. Arnel II S. Bernal, Public Information Officer.
The induction signals a renewed commitment to strengthening collaboration, advancing professional partnerships, and supporting quality teacher education across Region XI. As they begin their term, the new ARTEI XI leadership is poised to work collectively with member institutions and stakeholders in pursuing initiatives that further strengthen teacher education in the region.
